Introduction

The two pictures illustrate the main changes which have taken place in the center of a city named Mangon between the year 1700 and today.

Overview

Overall, it can be seen that Margon has become a much more developed city, with far more residential and transport facilities.

Body-1

One of the most noticeable changes is the modernization of the road system. Traffic lights, a roundabout and a pedestrian crossing have been added to improve traffic management. In the center of the town, both the church and the market square have been preserved since 1700. Residential areas have expanded considerably, with more houses being built along Market Street and Steggle Road.

Body-2
In the northern part of the town, Goode Farm has been demolished and replaced by a car park. Meanwhile, Steggle Farm in the south-west has been removed to make way for a public park, although the bridge within this area has remained unchanged. Furthermore, the number of shops has increased from three to five. In the north-east, some older houses have been replaced by additional housing and a block of flats, while the row of shops in the southern area has been extended.
